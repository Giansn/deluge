# Seed 152: E170 at a song swap, a synth without a ParamManager (patch 0112), 02.10.2026

Stop run 4's seed 152 (`--mode deep`, OLED, all 27 patches) froze with E170 at input 954: `SELECT_ENC` in the song browser, loading SONG001 while playing. The freeze is `Output::getParamManager()`'s check (`src/deluge/model/output.cpp:220`): an Output with no active Clip and no ParamManager backed up for it. Replays froze at the same input.

## Finding

The backtrace: the audio routine's launch event → `PlaybackHandler::doSongSwap()` → `Song::stopAllAuditioning()` on the old song (`playback_handler.cpp:1451`) → `MelodicInstrument::stopAnyAuditioning()` → `Output::getParamManager()`.

A hook at that call read the Output. It was a synth, `PETTRA ARP`, in the old song's main list, with no active Clip and no Clip instances. `notesAuditioned` was empty, so there was nothing to stop.

How the synth was left without a ParamManager:
- **The thinning:** loading while playing, the old song plays on until the launch event. `Song::deleteSoundsWhichWontSound()` (`song.cpp:2435`) frees RAM first. It deletes the Clips not active on their Output and keeps those Outputs. Then it deletes the Clips that won't sound, with their Outputs. Last, it deletes every ParamManager backed up with a Clip (`deleteAllBackedUpParamManagersWithClips()`).
- **The call:** `MelodicInstrument::stopAnyAuditioning()` (`melodic_instrument.cpp:506`) set up its model stack with `getParamManager()` before it looked at `notesAuditioned`. So it asked every synth, MIDI and CV Instrument for one, even with nothing to stop. `Kit::stopAnyAuditioning()` (`kit.cpp:1901`) only touches drums that are auditioned.
- **Not pinned down:** how exactly this synth came to have no Clip and no backup. Its ParamManager was gone after the thinning. Two short scenarios didn't recreate it: a Clip cloned and the original made MIDI; an arranger row turned CV and back to SYNTH. Both kept a ParamManager. A traced replay of inputs 940-954 saw no change to this synth: no new Output, no backup, no type change. It was in the song before that, and by the swap its ParamManager was gone.

A user gets there by loading a song while playing after editing tracks. This is a real freeze: FREEZE_WITH_ERROR stops a release build too, until SELECT is pressed. 1.2.1 has the same `stopAnyAuditioning()` and E170 check.

## Fix: patch 0112

`stopAnyAuditioning()` asks for the ParamManager only when notes are auditioned, as the kit does. It still clears the early notes and tells the active Clip to expect an event. The patch changes 12 lines.

What it doesn't change: a synth with no Clip and no ParamManager would still freeze on any other call to `getParamManager()`, for example an arranger audition pad. Here the old song is deleted right after the swap, and no other call reached it in the replays.
