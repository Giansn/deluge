# Seed 114: "bbbb" in the audio recorder is a harness artifact (rig13 models the receive DMA now), 01.10.2026

Campaign 2's seed 114 (`--mode deep`, OLED, 62a516c2 with 0001-0012, 0101-0105) froze with bbbb at input 86. The input was RECORD in the audio recorder (RECORD AUDIO from the sound editor). The whole series (0001-0016, 0101-0103, 0105, 0106) froze the same way.

## Finding

The replay traced `SampleRecorder::feedAudio()` and watched writes to `AudioEngine::i2sRXBufferPos`:
- **How the recorder is fed:** `doSomeOutputting()` feeds a recorder that records an input from its `sourcePos` up to `i2sRXBufferPos` (`src/deluge/processing/engines/audio_engine.cpp:1237-1254`). If `i2sRXBufferPos` is below `sourcePos`, it takes that for a wrap and reads to the end of the ring.
- **What moves the read position:** `AudioEngine::slowRoutine()` (`:1534-1549`) keeps `i2sRXBufferPos` 128 to 256 samples behind where the receive DMA writes (its CRDA, `getRxBufferCurrentPlace()`, `drivers/ssi/ssi.c:54`).
- **In the emulator:** that CRDA stood still, because song_emu models only the transmit DMA. So `slowRoutine()` pulled `i2sRXBufferPos` back by 128 samples every few milliseconds (0x4c00bc20 → 0x4c00b820, 0x4c00be00 → 0x4c00ba00, …).
- **The freeze:** the recorder, now ahead, read round the whole ring: 4 × 256 + 68 samples to the end, then 3 × 256 from the start. Level with the read position again, it was fed 0 samples, and that freezes with bbbb (`model/sample/sample_recorder.cpp:941`).

On the Deluge both DMAs run off the SSI's clock in step, so the read position stays in its window and isn't pulled back. This is a harness artifact, not a firmware bug.

## Fix (harness)

`tests/rig13.py` now models the receive DMA's CRDA. It starts 192 samples ahead of where the firmware reads and advances at the sample rate, on the transmit DMA's clock (LightDma).

Seed 114 was replayed on the whole series with it. Inputs 80-85 are the same as before. Input 86 (`CROSS held + RECORD`) goes through, and up to 130 there is no problem.

A thought for hardware, not seen: `doSomeOutputting()` can't tell a recorder ahead of the read position from one a lap behind. After a stall longer than the receive ring (2048 samples, 46 ms), the read position jumps by whole windows and could land behind a recorder too.

## Status
