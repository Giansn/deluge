# A fast zoom turn zooms the wrong way and past its limits (patch 0109), 01.10.2026

After 0016 and 0107, both regressions of the encoder overhaul #4529, the code was searched for more places that take a turn for ±1. One more turned up: the zoom in the clip views. The fuzzer hadn't hit it yet. The emulator confirmed it.

## Finding

The cause is in `TimelineView::horizontalEncoderAction()` (`src/deluge/gui/views/timeline_view.cpp:164-197`), which zooms when the horizontal encoder turns with its button held:
- **The input:** v1.3 hands a fast turn on as several detents at once (`src/deluge/hid/encoder_input.cpp:102-114`). 1.2.1 limited every turn to ±1 (`limitedDetentPos`).
- **The direction:** `zoomMagnitude = -offset` (`:165`). Only −1 zooms in (`:166`), so +2 or more zooms *out*.
- **The limits:** they are checked only for ±1 (`:188-189`). Any faster turn doubles `xZoom` with no limit.
- **The overflow:** `xZoom` is a `uint32_t`. After kMaxZoom (100,663,296) it passes 3,221,225,472, then reaches 0, and stays there: doubling and halving 0 gives 0. The clip views can't zoom any more. Divisions by `xZoom` return 0 on the Deluge (`__aeabi_idiv0`).

A user gets there with ordinary inputs: hold the horizontal encoder and turn it quickly. The views affected are the instrument clip view, the audio clip view, the automation view and the note row editor. The arranger zooms in its own code, one step by the sign (`arranger_view.cpp:2733-2747`), so it isn't affected. This is a real bug, not a harness artifact.

A song saved with zoom 0 loads with 1 (`song.cpp:1574`), so the damage doesn't stay in the file.

## Fix: patch 0109

The zoom takes one step in the turn's direction, as the arranger does: `zoomMagnitude = (offset > 0) ? -1 : 1`. A turn of ±1 behaves as before, and the limit checks hold again. The patch changes 1 line and adds 2 lines of comment.
