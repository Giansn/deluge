# "New Sitar Grii 10" with v17 and the real task scheduling, 27.09.2026

## Result

- **v17 lowers the load of this song to less than half:** monitor 93 → 41%, device estimate 117 → 47%. The reason is the bigger blocks (9.3 → 60 samples per render). The effort per call comes up much less often. The five kits drop from 38 to 14% of the time.
- **The price:** The reserve drops from 90 to 77 samples. There was no underrun and no QL.
- **The threshold 500 is too tight:** If the window load rises above 50%, v17 switches the minimum window off and tips into blocks of 12. At 0.8 instructions per cycle the load then jumps from 52 to 87%. With **650** the minimum window stays, and the reserve drops by only 2 samples. 800 brings nothing more here, but costs 5 samples.

## Setup

- **v16:** the rebuild, bit-identical with the release (`7eed1a77…`).
- **v17:** `patches/0001–0064` (dev branch `f563c24`), applied as for the rebuild. HEAD `4e3d2075`, `1.2.1-mastertune-v17-4e3d2075`, `deluge.bin` SHA-256 `d1ff492a…389fe5`. I built the variants with `kMinWindowMaxLoadPermille` 650 and 800 only locally (`-dirty`).
- **Measurement:** `sitar_emu.py --tasks`, the song as in `2026-09-27-sitar-emulator.md` (432 Hz, all kits).
  - **Task manager:** The firmware's task manager runs (`run_task_manager`, DMA in real time). So the firmware chooses its blocks itself, and direness and culling come from the firmware as on the device.
  - **CPU monitor:** on, the firmware computes the display itself (`cpu_stats::summarize()`).
  - **Card:** typical, 1 ms per command and 12 MB/s. Waits yield to the task manager.
  - **Duration:** 1 bar of lead-in, 4 bars measured (6.86 s).
  - **Method:** The values come from `sdload_emu.Run.run_tasks()`. The firmware measures the time per track itself (`profiler::outputTicks`).
- **Device estimate:** the audio routine's instructions at 400 MHz plus 60 ns (24 cycles) per SDRAM row, relative to the measured time. The rows per call come from `Run.lines()` right after the measurement (v17 0.1 s, v16 0.02 s). With v16, unicorn with memory hooks crashed after a longer time, the known problem from `sdload`.
- **Reserve** = 128 minus the biggest DMA gap in samples, as in `OPTIMIZATION.md` 7m.
- **Slower CPU:** In addition 0.8 and 0.5 instructions per cycle (`set_instructions_per_cycle`, like `sdload` s4ipc), to model a slower CPU. These runs are without row counting.

## Threshold of the minimum window (v17 only)

| 0.8 instructions per cycle | v16 | v17 (500) | v17 (650) | v17 (800) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CPU monitor, mean (peak) | 95.2% (163) | 87.2% (163) | **51.7%** (99) | 51.7% (116) |
| samples per render | 13.1 | 14.5 (mostly 12) | 59.6 | 59.6 |
| computing calls/s | 3,365 | 3,042 | 740 | 740 |
| the five kits | 37.2% | 33.7% | 17.1% | 17.1% |
| biggest gap | 53 = 1.20 ms | 55 = 1.25 ms | 57 = 1.29 ms | 60 = 1.36 ms |
| reserve | 75 | 73 | **71** | 68 |
| QL, culls because of load, underruns | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

- **At 1 instruction per cycle:** All three thresholds behave the same. The window load is around 41%, the minimum window always applies. Biggest gap 51, 51 and 47 samples, reserve 77, 77 and 81.
- **At 0.8 instructions per cycle, 500 tips over:**
  - The measured window load lies just above 50%. The minimum window goes off, and the routine computes in blocks of 12 again.
  - Every 1/8 s the minimum window applies again for a single block (`kWindowLoadMaxAge`). That block's load is above 50% again, so it goes off again.
  - The monitor swings between 72 and 95% meanwhile. With 650 and 800 it stays stable at 50–54%.
- **At 0.5 instructions per cycle everything is saturated:**
  - Monitor 98%, QL all the time (level 14), 53–57 samples per render.
  - Reserve v16 9, v17 6, 15 and 11 samples, no underrun.
  - The threshold has no effect here, because direness above 0 switches the minimum window off anyway. v16 and v17 are level, but v17 goes over a gap of 64 samples less often (186–193 against 390 times).

## Conclusions

- **Threshold:** 650 instead of 500. The device estimate for v17 is 47%, just below 500. Even small peaks would make v17 tip into the small blocks on the device. 650 keeps the minimum window at this load and costs 2 samples of reserve. 800 brings no further gain here, but costs 5 samples.
- **Alternative:** a hysteresis, i.e. off above 650 and on again below 500.
- **No culling because of load:** In no run were voices cut because of the load, not even at 0.5 instructions per cycle. On the device v16 cut 173 voices in 12 s. So the device is at times even slower, or more work comes in (UI, card, USB).
- **To clarify on the device:** measure v17 with this song on the device, ideally also with a build with threshold 650.
