What the numbers mean

Samples: 1000 times a second the Deluge looks where in the code it is. A function's share of the samples is its share of the computing time.

By track: the computing time of every track, measured exactly on the device, as a share of the time. It shows which track costs how much, even when it is silent.

Song::renderAudio: While a track computes, the interrupts are disabled. The samples of that time land collected in Song::renderAudio. Which track it was is under "By track". Which functions work inside it shows the profile in the emulator (tests/song).

Audio routine: share of the time in AudioEngine::routine. When idle it fills the free time with very small blocks. That is busy waiting, not overload.

Lost: samples that didn't reach the computer (USB too full). A few do no harm.
