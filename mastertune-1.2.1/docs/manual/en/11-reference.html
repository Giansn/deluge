11.6 Troubleshooting

Find your problem in its group. Each line gives the cause and what to do.

Installing and settings

The Deluge doesn't install the firmware.Leave only one .bin in the root folder and hold SHIFT while switching on (1.3).
It crashes or behaves strangely.Install deluge-1.2.1-mastertune-v18-124aeaa2.bin, v18 without L2, and report what happened (11.8).
The master tune is back at the old value after switching off.It is saved when you leave the Settings menu. Leave it with BACK first (2.1).
Output limiter, crossing guard and OLED brightness are back at their defaults.You switched to v17 or 1.2.1 and back; they lose settings they don't know. Set them again (7.6).

Tuning

An external synth doesn't follow the master tune.It must evaluate RPN 1 (Channel Fine Tuning). A device switched on later gets the tuning after the next play start (2.4).
A recording made at 432 Hz sounds off after editing it on the computer.Some editors remove the mtun chunk; the copy then counts as 440 Hz material. Use the original recording (2.3).

Load

QL or VC appear.The Deluge can't keep up. See what costs CPU in 3.5: at 432 Hz convert the samples with DelugeTuner; use fewer living drone tones.
The CPU monitor shows 85–90% while nothing plays.Up to v16 that was busy waiting, not overload. From v17 on the monitor shows the real load (3.4).
The first hit of an unused kit row comes late.The Kit RAM saver released its sample start. Switch it off if this bothers you (3.3).

USB

The computer doesn't show the audio input.Switch USB audio on, restart, and connect the Deluge as a USB device, not as a host (4.2).
USB audio is off again after a restart.Up to v15 the setting was saved only when you left the menu with BACK. From v16 on it is saved at once.
The Deluge isn't recognised or hangs with USB audio.Unplug the USB cable, start the Deluge, switch USB audio off (4.6).
The DAW lost the Deluge's MIDI ports.With USB audio on, the computer sees a new device. Assign the ports again (4.6).
UGAP appears.A 1 ms gap under very high load. Please report it (4.6).
deluge-editor says "needs community 1.3.0 or later".The Deluge reports 1.2.1. Ignore it: opening and saving work (8.7).
A file with umlauts in its name doesn't open over USB.Use only A–Z, 0–9 and _ in names (8.7).

Sound

A song with routing Parallel is quieter.Since v18 the parallel sum has half the volume, −6 dB. Raise the song's volume (5.2).
The filter whistles, or a tone follows the cutoff knob.The resonance is so high that the filter oscillates by itself. Turn the resonance down (5.3).
An old song's reverb sounds stiller.Songs without the new settings load with Modulation 0. Set Modulation to 50 for the old sound (5.10).
Echoes bend in pitch when the delay time changes.Time change is on Tape; old songs with a modulated or automated delay rate load that way. Choose Fade (5.11).
Hard sidechain pumping sounds softer.Since v18 ducking takes at least 5 ms (5.7).
The output clips.Only the sum at the output clips, at 0 dBFS. Lower the song volume until the peak lies at −6 dBFS, or switch the Output limiter on (5.8).
A click when switching the Output limiter.The output moves once by 0.7 ms. This is expected (5.8).

Tools

Windows warns when you start a tool.The programs are not signed. Click More info, then Run anyway (8.1).
DelugeRec names the take "… 1.2.1 v18.WAV" with v18.3.DelugeRec v6 cuts the version at the dot. Use v7 or later (8.2).
The profiler shows wrong function names.Load the .symbols.json that lies next to the .bin you installed (8.6).

11.8 Reporting bugs

mastertune is a personal fork. Don't report its bugs to the community developers. Report them at github.com/Giansn/deluge/issues, with:

  • the version from Settings → Firmware version;
  • what you did, exactly;
  • what the display showed: frozen, a restart, or a message like "E…";
  • for load problems, if you can, a CSV from the CPU monitor page (8.5) or a .jsonl recording from the profiler (8.6).
