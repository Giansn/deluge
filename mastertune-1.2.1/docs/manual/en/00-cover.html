The manual for firmware 1.2.1-mastertune v18.3: everything it changes and adds, with the frequency drone in full

mastertune builds on the official Deluge community firmware 1.2.1 and adds a master tune, a lighter audio engine, a CPU monitor, the Deluge as a USB audio interface, cleaner filters and effects, the arpeggiator from 1.3 and a frequency drone with up to 16 tones. This manual explains each change, where to find it on the Deluge and how to use it. Everything not described here works as in 1.2.1.

Written with Claude (AI). mastertune is a personal fork, not a contribution to the official firmware. Please don't report its bugs to the community developers; report them at github.com/Giansn/deluge/issues. Every change is tested in an emulator that runs the firmware's real ARM code, but v18.x has not been played on a real Deluge yet. Keep a copy of your SD card.
