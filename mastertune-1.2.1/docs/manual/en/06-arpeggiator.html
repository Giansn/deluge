6. Arpeggiator

mastertune has the complete arpeggiator of community firmware 1.3, with the menus in the familiar 1.2.1 style: one list, no horizontal menu. New items sit where they belong by topic. On top come latch, ratchet bounce and the ping-pong roll, which 1.3 doesn't have. v5 v6 v13

6.2 Latch

With Arpeggiator → Latch on, the arp keeps playing after you let go of the keys. The next note you play after releasing all keys replaces the held notes, without the arp falling out of time. Switching Latch off stops the held notes.

6.3 Ratchets, bounce and roll

SettingValuesWhat it does
Ratchet notes v6Auto, 2 … 8, RollAuto, as in 1.3: randomly 2, 4 or 8 hits, weighted by Number of ratchets. 2 … 8: always exactly that many; whether a step ratchets is then decided only by Ratchet probability. Roll v13: as many hits as it takes until the span is used up (below).
Ratchet bounce−10 … +10The hits of a ratchet like a bouncing ball. Each gap is r times the one before: r = 0.95 at +1, 0.8 at +4, 0.7 at +6, 0.5 at +10. Positive values get faster, negative ones slower. 0 = even, as before.
Bounce length v131 … 16Over how many arp steps one ratchet lasts. The note holds, then the arp continues with the next note. Rhythm and sequence length stay in time.
Bounce velocity v13Even, Fade, RiseEven: all hits equally loud. Fade (default): the faster, the quieter, like a ball. Rise: the faster, the louder, like ping-pong. It was called "Bounce fade" (on/off) in v6.
Ratchet probability0 … 100%How often a step ratchets.
  • Short syncs: with a fixed number of ratchet notes, sync 1/128 and 1/256 allow at most 2, sync 1/64 at most 4. Otherwise the gaps would hardly be longer than an audio block (2.9 ms).
  • Even ratchets divide the step by the number of notes. For 2, 4 and 8 that gives exactly the same times as before.
  • Compatible: with the defaults (Auto, Fade), Bounce length 1 and without Roll, the arp plays exactly as in v5 and v12.

Negative bounce plays the same pattern backwards: the hits start dense and spread out.

6.4 The ping-pong presets

Two synth presets show the roll. Copy PINGPONG ROLL.XML and PETTRA PINGPONG.XML from mastertune-1.2.1/presets/SYNTHS/ into the SYNTHS folder of your card. Load them in a synth clip like any other preset; the arp settings come with them. They need v13 or later.

The presets store no tempo. Pettra runs at 138 BPM; there the three hits fall 0, 99 and 169 ms after the start of the eighth.

  • A fill only now and then: lower the Ratchet probability. Or, in automation view, set it only on the steps before which the fill should come, and 0 elsewhere.
  • Longer or shorter: change Bounce length or Sync. At 1/16 and Bounce length 8 the fill also lasts half a note.
  • Softer or harder acceleration: Ratchet bounce +1 … +10.
  • The hit after the fill should bang: set Ratchet probability below 100%. The landing step is then a normal note at full velocity. At 100% the next fill starts right there, and Rise starts it at half velocity.

6.5 Randomizer

The randomizer has its own menu right after Arpeggiator:

  • Lock: repeatable randomness over 16 steps.
  • Spreads: gate, octave and velocity.
  • Chord polyphony and probability.
  • Probabilities for note, swap, bass, glide and reverse.

Reverse probability plays single arp notes with their sample backwards. Unlike in 1.3 this applies per voice, so notes sounding at the same time don't reverse each other.

6.6 Songs, presets and compatibility

  • 1.2.1 songs and presets load unchanged, including MIDI and CV tracks with arp settings.
  • Stock 1.2.1 loads mastertune files but skips the new entries (kit arp, randomizer, latch, bounce) and loses them at the next save. Walk and Pattern become Up there.
  • Automation view: the internal parameter numbers now match those of 1.3. This only changes which parameter is preselected when you first open an old song in automation view. Values, automations, mod knob and MIDI learn assignments are saved by name and are not affected.
  • Same start every time: in 1.2.1 LFOs, mod FX and the arp started at a random point after loading. Now they always start the same way. v13
  • deluge-editor doesn't show the arp additions (§8.7). Set them on the Deluge; they are kept in the file when the editor saves.

Not taken over from 1.3: the pad shortcuts in column 15 (velocity spread, Lock, Note probability), because 1.2.1 uses these pads for patching; and the MIDI Follow CCs for arp parameters, because they would have shifted existing assignments.
