7. Songs and workflow

A song browser that keeps a song's versions together, a countdown when you change songs, saving without crackle, a dimmer OLED and pads that don't flicker, plus fixes for MIDI, clock and the card.

7.1 Song browser with versions v17, v18

Songs whose names differ only by a number at the end are versions of one song: "New Sitar Grii", "New Sitar Grii 2" … "New Sitar Grii 10" appear as one line with an arrow. The line shows the whole name.

A number after "-" or "." belongs to the name: "TR-808" and "Jam 2026-09-27" stay songs of their own.

To …do this
open a groupClick: press SELECT or LOAD. The versions appear indented below it.
load a versionTurn to it and click. This also works during playback, with the countdown (§7.2).
close a groupBACK on one of its versions. Groups you opened stay open while you keep turning, up to 8.
find the loaded songOpen the browser: the group of the loaded song is already open.
load by nameType the name; it loads directly.
deleteSHIFT + SAVE on a version. On a closed group line it is blocked: "Open the group first".

Saving and the other browsers are unchanged. The browser reads the folder less often than before, and while you browse during playback the audio routine pauses for at most 0.39 ms.

7.2 Song change with a countdown v14

Load a song while another one is playing, and the Deluge counts down to the change:

  • as long as repeats remain, it counts the loops;
  • in the last loop the bars;
  • in the last bar the beats, 4-3-2-1.

It stays right with swing and with an external MIDI clock.

  • OLED: the countdown stays in the title line, for example "Bars remaining 3".
  • 7-segment display: the number, blinking while it counts loops. Beats carry a dot, so you can tell them from bars.
  • Until the change the gold knobs and mod buttons control the master effects of the playing song, from the moment you press LOAD, even with Affect Entire off. The new song keeps its own values.
The countdown has priority v19.0.2. While a countdown runs, nothing covers it:
  • OLED: it stays on the title line, drawn over everything else. A popup, such as a gold knob's value, the filter type or the DJ filter, appears below it.
  • In Song view too: when clips or a section are armed to launch, the countdown (the bars until the launch, in the last bar the beats) is on the title line. Before, it was a box in the middle of the screen, which every knob covered; now the song's name stays visible below it.
  • The record count-in v19.0.3: when you record with a count-in (Settings → Recording → Count-in bars, then REC and PLAY), the beats until the recording begins are on the title line too ("Count-in 4", 3, 2, 1), in every view, instead of a popup in the middle that any knob took the place of. It is gone the moment the recording begins.
  • The CPU monitor's line moves to the bottom while a countdown takes the top.
  • 7-segment display: the gold knobs and mod buttons show no popup while it shows a countdown, the count-in's number included. They still do what they do.

7.3 Saving without crackle v11, v13

  • No crackle: in 1.2.1 the audio engine stood still while the Deluge wrote a song or preset to the card. The output repeated its last buffer: a short crackle at every save. Now the audio keeps running, and the samples that are playing keep loading. This applies to songs, synth and kit presets and settings.
  • Faster: saving during playback takes 22 instead of 344 ms in the emulator (a song with 3 synths), or 66 instead of 716 ms (5 synths).
  • No loading window while you browse: a small animation runs at the top right of the OLED while loading and saving, as in community firmware 1.3. The preset name stays visible.

7.4 OLED brightness v18

Settings → OLED brightness, levels 1–10. 10 is as bright as before, 1 is still readable. Each turn takes effect at once, and the level applies again after a restart. The readable firmware version is described in §1.4.

7.5 Flicker-free pad dimming v13

Settings → Community features → Flicker-free dimming, on by default.

  • Why the pads flickered: 1.2.1 dims the pads through the LED controller with dark pauses. Below 40% brightness the pauses get so long that the whole refresh slows down, 6.8 times at 0%.
  • Now: the LED controller always refreshes at full speed and dims only down to 43.5%. The firmware dims the rest through the colours of the pads, the sidebar and the gold knob LEDs. Every level gives the same amount of light as before.
  • Trade-off: the button LEDs, the 7-segment display and pads that the LED controller blinks by itself (the fast play cursor, blinking shortcuts in menus) dim only down to 43.5%. At very low brightness they are brighter than before.
  • Off sends byte for byte the same as 1.2.1.

7.6 Settings that survive v18.3

When the Deluge saves its community settings, it now keeps those it doesn't know, for example from a newer version, with their name and value. 1.2.1, v17 and the current community firmware wrote them back under a wrong name. So switching to v17 and back still resets Output limiter, Filter crossing guard and OLED brightness to their defaults. Set them again after such a switch.

7.7 MIDI, clock and card fixes

FixSinceWhat changed
MIDI and gate outputs on timev13Events late in an audio block went out a whole block (2.9 ms) too early, especially while saving. And the timer of the MIDI and gate outputs was never reset before the start: a run could come up to 38 samples early, or after an overflow about 127 ms late. Now every run is accurate to ±1.4 samples.
Late clock bytesv17A clock byte that is read late now ticks at once instead of up to 2.9 ms late.
Clock outputs under external clockv15When the Deluge followed an external MIDI clock, its gate clock and MIDI clock outputs stopped after a short burst. Now both keep running in time with the incoming clock. With the internal clock everything stays as before.
Section launch by CCv15A section launch learned to a CC was lost when the song was loaded; learned notes were kept. Now the CC is kept too.
USB MIDI from the computerv15If MIDI came faster than once per millisecond (dense automation from the DAW, SysEx, file access over USB), packets were lost. Now the computer waits until the Deluge is ready.
Card transfersv14Around every transfer from and to the SD card, a write happening at the same time right next to the buffer could get lost. This bug is from 1.2.1.
File access over USBv14In rare cases wrong bytes could end up in a copied file (§8.7).

The three fixes of v15 were taken over from the current community firmware.
